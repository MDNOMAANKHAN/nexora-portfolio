06 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we
start building.

A few helpful details about how a website project can work with Nexora.

What kind of businesses do you work with?＋

We focus on local businesses such as clinics, restaurants, retail shops, fitness centres and service providers. Tell us about your business and we can discuss a suitable solution.

Can you build a custom web application?＋

Yes. Depending on the requirements, this can include booking flows, dashboards, portals and business management tools. Features and scope are agreed before development begins.

How long does a website take?＋

Timelines depend on the number of pages, features, content and feedback. We discuss a realistic delivery schedule after understanding the project.

Do you provide domain, hosting and maintenance?＋

We can discuss domain and hosting setup, deployment and ongoing maintenance as part of your project quotation. The exact inclusions are agreed in advance.

How much does a project cost?＋

Pricing depends on the design, functionality and scope. Share your requirements and we can prepare a project-specific quotation.
